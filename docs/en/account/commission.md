# Commission transfers and withdrawals

Open [Referral Program](https://oixcloud.com/user/invite) and select **Withdraw or transfer to balance**, or go directly to [Withdraw Commission](https://oixcloud.com/user/ticket/referral_program). Check your available commission and enter the **amount in CNY**.

## Transfer to account balance {#balance}

**Transferred funds can only be spent on the account and cannot be withdrawn.** The minimum is ¥0.01 and there is no fee. Under **Transfer to Balance**, select **Confirm Transfer**, check the amount and verify with a passkey or your current password. Check the updated balance and remaining commission.

## Withdraw USDT {#withdraw}

1. With an active plan, enter at least ¥200. Choose an available **TRC-20** or **Polygon** channel, enter a USDT receiving address on that same network, and select **Submit Request**.
2. Before confirming, check the network, full receiving address, fee and amount you will receive. The current fee is ¥20 for TRC-20 and ¥0 for Polygon. If your account balance is negative, commission covers the debt first. Only the remainder is available to withdraw. The confirmation shows the debt offset and total commission used.
3. Verify with a passkey or your current password. In **Withdrawal History** on the same page, check the status, USDT amount and transaction hash when available. If the result is unclear, check history before trying again.
