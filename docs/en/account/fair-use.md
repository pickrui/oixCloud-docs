# Fair use

## Connections, sharing, and fair use

Personal plans are for your own devices and must not be lent, jointly purchased, shared, or resold. Team use must follow the product's stated terms. A connection threshold is not a limit on the number of signed-in devices. Excessive concurrent connections, scanning, attacks, and other abuse may trigger speed limits or account restrictions. Stop abnormal traffic and check for recovery. If the issue persists, submit a ticket with the time and node details.
