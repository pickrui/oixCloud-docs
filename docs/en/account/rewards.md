# Reward links and purchase gifts

Sign in to the [Benefits Center](https://oixcloud.com/user/benefits#reward-links) and find **Reward Links**. Claim a reward plan for yourself or generate a single-use link for a friend without spending benefit credits. Available campaigns, plans, quantities and eligibility follow the page.

## Which purchases earn gifts {#eligibility}

1. Expand the campaign's **Eligibility**. Check **Qualifying purchases**, billing periods, gifts per purchase, the per-account campaign limit and whether automatic renewals qualify.
2. Complete an actual paid purchase of the specified plan and period while the campaign is enabled, following the [purchase steps](/en/account/plans#purchase). Manual renewal uses the same purchase entry.
3. Return to **Reward Links** and check available gifts, remaining capacity and the generated count before choosing to claim or share.

Only purchases meeting that campaign's rules earn slots. Adding account balance alone does not earn a purchase gift. Zero-cost orders, free claims, upgrades/switches and admin grants do not earn new slots. Automatic renewals qualify only when that campaign allows them; purchases made before it was enabled are not credited retroactively.

Earned slots do not require the original plan to remain active. Some other campaigns use your current active plan and a purchase made before the campaign began; check each campaign's **Eligibility**.

## Reward plan and gift period {#reward-plan}

A campaign can offer a fixed plan or show **Same plan as the original purchase**. A matching-plan gift follows the order that earned its slot, while **the gift period is preset separately**. For example, buying Gold annually can earn a monthly Gold gift, and buying Silver annually can earn a monthly Silver gift. An annual purchase does not necessarily earn a full year as a gift.

Upgrading the original order or changing your current account plan does not change an earned gift's plan. If one campaign has several available gift plans, currently available slots are used in purchase order. Check available gifts and plan duration before generating links. Slots for a temporarily unavailable reward are retained, while other available gifts can still be used.

## Claim for yourself {#claim-self}

::: warning Check original-order refund plans first
Claiming for yourself counts as a claimed gift. Once completed, the original purchase no longer offers self-service refunds and requires support. Read the [refund rules](#refunds) before claiming if you intend to refund that order.
:::

1. Select **Claim 1 for myself** in the intended campaign.
2. Check the reward plan, gift duration and confirmation, then confirm to use one slot.
3. Check [Plan Management](https://oixcloud.com/user/bought). If you already have an active plan, the reward is queued as Pending; otherwise it activates immediately. Pending plans follow the [queue and activation rules](/en/account/plans#manual-activation), without immediately adding data or time to your current plan.

A failed claim does not consume a slot. If the connection fails or the result is unclear, check plan records first, then return to the original campaign to retry. Do not keep claiming additional gifts to check the result.

## Generate a link for a friend {#share}

**Each link can be claimed once, and anyone holding it can claim it.** Send it only to the intended recipient; keep it out of public chats, screenshots and public documents. Once claimed, the gift cannot be reset, deleted or returned to the slot pool.

1. Choose **Quantity** in the campaign and select **Generate claim link**. If fewer slots are available, use the actual generated result.
2. Expand **Your generated links**. Check each link's reward plan, status and **Claim Deadline**, then copy and send it to your friend.
3. The recipient opens the link and signs in or registers the account intended to receive the gift. Return to and refresh the claim page, check the account and plan, then select **Confirm Plan Claim**.
4. The recipient checks the Active or Pending record in Plan Management. The sender can check whether the original campaign shows the link as **Claimed**.

A link is valid for 365 days after generation. This is the claim deadline, **not the reward plan's duration**; check the campaign and claim page for that duration. Opening the page alone does not claim the gift: confirmation is required.

## Reset, delete and campaign end {#manage-links}

| Action or state | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Reset an unclaimed link | The old address stops working, a new address receives a new claim deadline, and the same slot remains used with the same reward plan. |
| Delete an unclaimed link | The old address stops working and its slot is returned. Generating again still requires campaign eligibility. |
| A link has been claimed | It cannot be reset or deleted. The slot is not returned if the recipient later deletes their account. |
| The campaign has ended | No new gift slots or links are issued, including direct self-claims. Existing links follow their own status and deadline; unclaimed links can still be reset or deleted. |

Before resetting, tell the recipient that the old address will stop working, then send the replacement. If the reward plan or period is unavailable, generation, reset or claiming may be unavailable; follow the page's reason.

## Original-order refunds and gifts {#refunds}

Before refunding, check both the [original order's self-service refund eligibility](/en/account/plans#refund) and its gift status.

| Gift status | What happens when the original order is refunded |
| --- | --- |
| Unclaimed, including generated links | Its gift slots are revoked and unclaimed links stop working. |
| Claimed by you or a friend | Self-service refunds stop. Contact support; a manual refund preserves the recipient's gift plan. |

Deleting the original order through the admin interface also revokes unclaimed gifts. If applying a coupon or switching a plan refunds all of the original paid amount to zero, the same unclaimed-revocation or claimed-support rule applies. Ordinary price adjustments that leave a positive paid amount do not earn new gifts.

When a refund and claim happen together, check the final outcome. If preparing a refund, ask the recipient to pause claiming. For manual review, provide the original purchase record, campaign name and relevant messages through [payment and plan support](/en/help/support#payment).

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| Symptom | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| No new slots after payment | Whether the plan purchase actually completed, the campaign was enabled, the plan and period qualify, and automatic renewal is included. |
| Generation unavailable | The per-account limit, available slots, campaign state and reward availability; follow the displayed reason. |
| Current data did not increase after a self-claim | Check whether the gift was queued as Pending and follow queue or activation rules. |
| A friend cannot open or claim a link | Whether the address was replaced by a reset, expired, already claimed or has an unavailable reward. |
| Refund action missing or support required | The original order's eligibility and whether any associated gift has already been claimed. |
