# Reward links and purchase gifts

Open **Reward Links** in the [Benefits Center](https://oixcloud.com/user/benefits#reward-links). Generate a link to claim the gift yourself or share it with a friend. Neither uses benefit credits.

## Which purchases earn gifts {#eligibility}

1. Expand the campaign's **Eligibility**. Check **Qualifying purchases**, billing periods, gifts per purchase, the per-account campaign limit and whether automatic renewals qualify.
2. Complete an actual paid purchase of the specified plan and period while the campaign is enabled, following the [purchase steps](/en/account/plans#purchase). Manual renewal uses the same purchase entry.
3. Return to **Reward Links** and check the available gifts, remaining slots and **Your generated links**.

Only purchases meeting that campaign's rules earn slots. Adding account balance alone does not earn a purchase gift. Zero-cost orders, free claims, upgrades/switches and admin grants do not earn new slots. Automatic renewals qualify only when that campaign allows them; purchases made before it was enabled are not credited retroactively.

Earned slots do not require the original plan to remain active. Some other campaigns use your current active plan and a purchase made before the campaign began; check each campaign's **Eligibility**.

## Reward plan and gift period {#reward-plan}

A campaign can offer a fixed plan or show **Same plan as the original purchase**. A matching-plan gift follows the order that earned its slot, while **the gift period is preset separately**. For example, buying Gold annually can earn a monthly Gold gift, and buying Silver annually can earn a monthly Silver gift. An annual purchase does not necessarily earn a full year as a gift.

A support-assisted change to the original order or switching your current account plan does not change an earned gift's plan. If one campaign has several available gift plans, currently available slots are used in purchase order. Check available gifts and plan duration before generating links. Slots for a temporarily unavailable reward are retained, while other available gifts can still be used.

## Claim for yourself {#claim-self}

::: warning Check original-order refund plans first
New orders are restricted as soon as gift credits are actually awarded, before generation or claiming. Historical orders keep their original rules: claiming for yourself counts as a claimed gift and then requires support for an original-order refund. Read the [refund rules](#refunds) first.
:::

1. Open **Reward Links** with the account that should receive the plan. Under **Your generated links**, open the **Claim page** for an unclaimed link. If you need a new link, select **Generate claim link** to create one, then open its claim page.
2. Check the signed-in account, reward plan and gift duration. Select **Confirm Plan Claim**. The link can be used once.
3. Check [Plan Management](https://oixcloud.com/user/bought). If you already have an active plan, the reward joins the [activation queue](/en/account/plan-changes#manual-activation). Otherwise, it starts immediately.

Generating a link uses a slot. If the confirmation result is unclear, check Plan Management and the original link’s status. Retry with the same link if it is still valid and unclaimed.

## Generate a link for a friend {#share}

**Each link can be claimed once, and anyone holding it can claim it.** Send it only to the intended recipient; keep it out of public chats, screenshots and public documents. Once claimed, the gift cannot be reset, deleted or returned to the slot pool.

1. Select **Generate claim link** in the campaign. If you have more than one slot left, you can choose the **Quantity** first.
2. Expand **Your generated links**. Check each link's reward plan, status and **Claim Deadline**, then copy and send it to your friend.
3. The recipient opens the link and signs in or registers the account intended to receive the gift. Return to and refresh the claim page, check the account and plan, then select **Confirm Plan Claim**.
4. The recipient checks the Active or Pending record in Plan Management. The sender can check whether the original campaign shows the link as **Claimed**.

A link is valid for 365 days after generation. This is the claim deadline, **not the reward plan's duration**; check the campaign and claim page for that duration. Opening the page alone does not claim the gift: confirmation is required.

## Reset, delete and campaign end {#manage-links}

| Action or state | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Reset an unclaimed link | The old address stops working, a new address receives a new claim deadline, and the same slot remains used with the same reward plan. |
| Delete an unclaimed link | The old address stops working and its slot is returned. Generating again still requires campaign eligibility. |
| A link has been claimed | It cannot be reset or deleted. The slot is not returned if the recipient later deletes their account. |
| Purchase gift campaign ended | New purchases stop earning slots. Previously awarded purchase slots can still generate links to use or share |
| Plan-based eligibility campaign ended | New link generation closes. In either type of campaign, existing valid, unclaimed links can still be used or shared, or reset or deleted subject to the displayed conditions |

Before resetting, tell the recipient that the old address will stop working, then send the replacement. If the reward plan or period is unavailable, generation, reset or claiming may be unavailable; follow the page's reason.

## Original-order refunds and plan changes {#refunds}

New orders that actually receive gift credits do not support self-service plan changes, upgrades, refunds to account balance or applying a coupon after purchase. This applies before links are generated or claimed. Other orders on the account are unaffected; normal usage, activation and renewal switches remain available. Check [Plan Management](/en/account/refund) and contact [support](/en/help/support#payment) for special cases.

| Order or gift state | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| New-rule order with awarded gift credits | The original order is restricted even while all gifts remain unclaimed; deleting links or ending the campaign does not remove the restriction |
| Historical order, gifts unclaimed | Original change/coupon rules remain; an otherwise eligible refund revokes its unclaimed slots and links |
| Historical order, any gift claimed | Original-order refunds require support; other actions retain their original rules |
| Support-assisted refund | Unclaimed gift credits and links are revoked; claimed gifts and the recipient's plan are retained |

A purchase can earn only the slots left in that purchase gift campaign. The cap counts both generated links and unused awarded slots. If the cap is full or the reward is unavailable, the purchase earns no slot and does not incur the associated order restrictions. Lowering the cap preserves slots already awarded. If their reward plan becomes unavailable, wait for it to return or contact support.

Coupon discounts at checkout and independently matching campaigns can still combine. A zero-paid purchase earns no new purchase gifts. The restriction concerns applying a coupon after purchase, which can refund a price difference. Historical orders retain their prior handling when such a settlement returns the entire paid amount to zero.

Before support handles a refund, provide the original purchase record, campaign name and related messages. Do not ask a recipient to claim while support is checking the order.

## Benefit draws and commission withdrawals

If a free draw awards another try, the next draw is free. A paid draw with that result returns the credits used. If the result is unclear, use **View last result** to check the original draw. See [UHD redemption and extensions](/en/account/uhd) to use benefit credits or [commission transfers and withdrawals](/en/account/commission) to use referral earnings.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| Symptom | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| No new slots after payment | Whether the plan purchase actually completed, the campaign was enabled, the plan and period qualify, and automatic renewal is included. |
| Generation unavailable | The per-account limit, available slots, campaign state and reward availability; follow the displayed reason. |
| Current data did not increase after a self-claim | Check whether the gift was queued as Pending and follow queue or activation rules. |
| A friend cannot open or claim a link | Whether the address was replaced by a reset, expired, already claimed or has an unavailable reward. |
| Refund action missing or support required | The original order's eligibility and whether any associated gift has already been claimed. |
