# Icon sources and licenses

The icons are single-color masks drawn in the current text color by `../icons.css`. Some files were trimmed for that use: titles and sizes removed, view boxes cropped to the drawing, colors or gradients dropped.

Trademarks, logos and product names belong to their respective owners. Platform and app marks are used only to show which platform or app a guide covers. They do not imply endorsement.
